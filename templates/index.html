🧼 Vegetable Washing Guide

Follow each step to clean vegetables properly.

Step 1: Wash Your Hands

Wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds before handling vegetables.

Clean hands first!

Step 2: Wash Vegetables

Rinse vegetables carefully under clean running water to remove visible dirt.

Remove visible dirt!

Step 3: Scrub Firm Vegetables

Use a clean vegetable brush to scrub firm vegetables like potatoes and carrots.

Scrub gently!

Step 4: Dry Vegetables

Pat vegetables dry with a clean paper towel or cloth.

Keep them clean and dry!

❄️ Vegetable Storage Guide

Learn how to store different vegetables properly.

🥬 Refrigerator Storage

Keep leafy vegetables in the refrigerator to maintain freshness.

🥕 Carrot Storage

Remove leafy tops and keep carrots refrigerated in a container.

🥔 Potato Storage

Store potatoes in a cool, dry and dark place.

🍅 Tomato Storage

Keep unripe tomatoes at room temperature until ripe.

🥗 Food Safety Guide

Good hygiene helps prevent germs from spreading during food preparation.

🧼 Hand Hygiene

Wash hands with soap and water before preparing food.

🔪 Separate Foods

Use separate boards and utensils for raw meat and vegetables.

✨ Clean Kitchen

Clean kitchen counters and utensils before and after use.

🥗 Fresh Food

Choose fresh vegetables and discard spoiled produce.
